# Sample answer sheet — source

- **Sheets:** `data/samples/page*.jpg` — original handwritten answer sheet(s), written by hand on A4 paper
  by the candidate and photographed on a phone (no suitable public Python-subject exam scripts were found
  quickly; the brief allows "your own handwritten sample").
- **Subject:** Python Programming — Core Concepts, 8 questions, 37 marks.
- **Question paper + short key:** [`QUESTION_PAPER.md`](QUESTION_PAPER.md)
- **Full rubric (what the scorer grades against):** [`rubrics/python_rubric.json`](rubrics/python_rubric.json) —
  every question is split into 1-mark criteria (Q8's example is worth 2); `needs_diagram` marks points that depend on a drawing.

The answers on the sheet are deliberately of mixed quality (complete, paraphrased, partial, blank)
so the scoring and confidence flags can be checked against a known expectation.
